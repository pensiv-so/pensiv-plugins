## Keep writing, or lose it all

**Dangerous Writing** turns your blank page into a high-stakes sprint. Arm your open document, then keep typing. The moment you pause for too long, a fuse burns down, and when it hits zero everything you've written is erased. There is no undo.

It's the antidote to the inner editor: when deleting your own words is off the table because _stopping_ deletes them for you, you stop second-guessing and just write.

Inspired by Squibler's [Dangerous Writing Prompt App](https://www.squibler.io/dangerous-writing-prompt-app), rebuilt natively for pensiv so it arms your real document, right where you write.

![Pick a goal and arm your document from the 🔥 toolbar popover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pensiv-so/pensiv-plugins/main/plugins/dangerous-writing/screenshots/01-launcher.png)

### Usage

1. Open a document and hit the 🔥 button in the toolbar.
2. Pick a goal: survive for a set **time**, or reach a **word count**.
3. Start writing, and keep the fuse alive by never pausing too long.
4. Reach your goal and your words are safe. Stall out and the page is wiped.

![The screen reddens as the fuse burns. Don't stop](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pensiv-so/pensiv-plugins/main/plugins/dangerous-writing/screenshots/02-session.png)

### Features

- **Two goals**: outlast a timer or hit a word target
- **Adjustable fuse**: set how long you can pause before the wipe (3–10s)
- **Hardcore mode**: a shorter fuse and almost no warning
- **Full-screen tension**: the screen reddens as the fuse burns

### ⚠️ Warning

This plugin **permanently erases your open document** if you stop typing too long. There is no undo. Use it on a scratch draft, not your only copy.
